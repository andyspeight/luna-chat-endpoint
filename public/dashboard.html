      Train Luna on Your Website
      
    
      Paste your website URLs below and Luna will read each page, learn your content, and use it to answer visitor questions accurately. The more pages you add, the smarter Luna gets about your business.
      

    
      
      
      Website Pages
      
      
      Add the pages you want Luna to learn from — your About page, destinations, FAQ, terms, pricing, ATOL info, anything a customer might ask about. One URL per line.
      
      
      
        
      
      
      
      
        
        
        
      
      
      
      Scanning reads the visible text content from each page. It does not access anything behind a login.
      
    
      

    
      
      
      Knowledge Sources
      
      
      Each page Luna has learned from. Rescan one if its content has changed, or remove pages you no longer want her to use.
      
      
      
      
      
        
      
          0 pages scanned
          
        
      
        
      
      
      
    
      

    
      
      
      Auto-Rescan
      
      
      When enabled, Luna will automatically rescan your pages weekly to pick up any content changes on your website.

      Business Type
      
      
      Select all services this client offers. The first type determines default home screen cards. Starters are combined from all selected types. You can override any defaults below.
      
      
      
    
      

    
      
      
      Welcome Message
      
      
      
        
        
      Shown in the home screen header when visitors open the widget.
      
      
      
    
      

    
      
      
      Quick Starters
      
      
      These appear as tappable pills on the home screen. Use prompts that show what your bot can do.
      
      
      
      One per line. Maximum 8.
      
    
      

    
      
      
      Home Screen Cards
      
      
      The three capability cards on the home screen. Each card has an icon, title, and description. Leave empty to use defaults (holiday search cards).
      
      
      
      
    
      

    
      
      
      Name Collection
      
      
      
      When enabled, visitors see a name/email form before the home screen.
      
    
      

    
      
      
      Holiday Search Types
      
      
      Select which search types Luna can offer visitors. You must select at least one for the holiday search feature to work.
      
      
      
    
      

    
      
      
      Privacy Policy
      
      
      
        
        
        
      A link to your privacy policy. Shown at the bottom of the chat sign-in screen.
      
      
      
    
      

    
      

    
    
      

    
      
      
      Auto-Trigger
      
      
      Automatically open the chat widget based on what visitors do — after a set time, when they scroll far enough, or on exit intent — optionally only on certain pages. The first condition met opens the chat.

      Multilingual Support
      
      
      When enabled, Luna automatically detects the visitor's language and responds in kind. Perfect for agents serving international customers.
      
      
      
        
      
      
      
      
        
      
          
          
          
      Leave empty to support all languages. Or list the specific languages you want Luna to respond in. If a visitor writes in an unsupported language, Luna will reply in English.
